WORK FROM HOME POLICY (2026 Edition)

1. Eligibility
- All employees who have completed their probation period (3 months) are eligible for hybrid work.
- Fully remote arrangements require Director-level approval and a signed remote work agreement.

2. Hybrid Schedule
- Standard hybrid schedule: 3 days in office, 2 days remote per week.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time on remote days.
- Remote days may be changed with 24 hours notice to the team lead.

3. Equipment & Stipend
- The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse.
- A one-time home-office setup stipend of $500 is available.
- A monthly internet stipend of $40 is reimbursed against receipts.
- Equipment remains company property and must be returned upon exit.

4. Security Requirements
- Employees must use the company VPN at all times when handling company data remotely.
- Two-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts.
- Sensitive documents must never be stored on personal devices.

5. Performance & Availability
- Employees must be reachable on the company chat during core hours.
- Managers conduct weekly 1:1s; one per month may be held remotely.
- Output-based evaluation applies; remote employees are measured on deliverables, not hours logged.

6. Tax & Location
- Employees working remotely from another state/country for more than 30 days per year must notify HR for payroll and tax compliance.
