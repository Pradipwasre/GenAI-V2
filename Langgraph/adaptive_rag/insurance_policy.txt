EMPLOYEE INSURANCE POLICY (2026 Edition)

1. Health Insurance
- Comprehensive medical coverage for employee, spouse, and dependent children.
- Annual deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family.
- Coinsurance: 80/20 in-network, 60/40 out-of-network.
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $4,000 individual / $8,000 family.
- Coverage begins on the first day of employment.

2. Dental & Vision
- Dental: preventive care 100% covered; major procedures 50% covered.
- Vision: $200 allowance for glasses/contacts every 24 months; annual eye exam covered.

3. Life & Disability
- Basic life insurance: 2x annual salary, employer-paid.
- Optional supplemental life up to 6x salary (employee-paid).
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks.
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary after 90 days of disability.

4. Mental Health
- Therapy and counseling covered at in-network rates.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): 8 free confidential counseling sessions per year per household member.

5. Enrollment & Changes
- Open enrollment: November 1 - November 30, effective January 1.
- Qualifying life events allow mid-year changes within 30 days.
