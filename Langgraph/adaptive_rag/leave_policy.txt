COMPANY LEAVE POLICY (2026 Edition)

1. Annual Leave
- All full-time employees are entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues at 1.75 days per completed month of service.
- Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried over into Q1 of the following year; the remainder lapses on March 31.
- Annual leave requests require manager approval at least 7 calendar days in advance.

2. Sick Leave
- Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.
- Sick leave for 1-2 consecutive days may be self-certified up to 3 times per year.
- Unused sick leave does not carry over.

3. Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks fully paid, extendable by 12 weeks unpaid.
- Paternity leave: 4 weeks fully paid.
- Adoption leave mirrors maternity/paternity entitlements.
- Parental leave must be applied for at least 4 weeks before the expected start date.

4. Public Holidays
- The company observes 12 public holidays per year. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed.

5. Unpaid Leave
- Up to 30 days of unpaid leave per year may be granted at management discretion.
- Unpaid leave does not count toward service time for leave accrual.

6. Leave Payout
- Accrued but unused annual leave is paid out upon resignation or termination.
- Sick leave is not paid out under any circumstances.
