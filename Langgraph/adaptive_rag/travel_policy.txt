BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY (2026 Edition)

1. Booking
- All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal.
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for flights over 6 hours.
- Business class is permitted for flights over 10 hours with VP approval.

2. Hotel Limits (per night, before tax)
- Tier 1 cities (New York, London, Tokyo, Singapore): $300
- Tier 2 cities: $220
- Other locations: $180
- Sharing accommodations is encouraged but not required.

3. Per Diem Allowances
- Meals: $75/day (Tier 1), $60/day (Tier 2), $50/day (other).
- Local transport: $40/day or use of company-approved ride services.
- Tips are covered within the per diem and not reimbursed separately.

4. Expense Reimbursement
- Expenses over $50 require itemized receipts.
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion.
- Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of approval.

5. Travel Insurance
- The company provides business travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and lost baggage.
- Coverage applies from departure until return.

6. Visa & Documentation
- The company covers visa fees and expedited processing for business travel.
- A valid passport with 6+ months validity is the employee's responsibility.
