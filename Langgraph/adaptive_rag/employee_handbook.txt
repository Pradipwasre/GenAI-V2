EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (2026 Edition)

1. Code of Conduct
- Employees must act with integrity, respect, and professionalism.
- Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately.

2. Working Hours
- Standard work week: Monday to Friday, 40 hours.
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM; flextime applies outside core hours.
- Overtime is not expected for salaried staff; hourly staff are compensated per local law.

3. Probation
- New hires serve a 3-month probation period.
- Performance review at the end of probation determines confirmation.

4. Compensation & Reviews
- Salaries are reviewed annually, effective April 1.
- Performance bonuses target 10-15% of annual salary, based on company and individual performance.
- Equity grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.

5. Remote & Hybrid Work
- Hybrid and remote arrangements follow the Work From Home Policy.
- The company operates on a flexible, output-driven model.

6. Grievance Procedure
- Grievances may be raised with HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.
- All grievances are acknowledged within 5 business days.

7. Termination & Notice
- Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.
- Severance of 2 weeks per year of service applies to company-initiated termination without cause.
- Final pay and accrued leave payout are processed within 30 days of the last working day.
